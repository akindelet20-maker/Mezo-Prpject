01

Bring your people

Create a circle and share its factory address and ID. Joining is open until every place is filled. Join order becomes payout order.

02

Show up for the round

Each member contributes the same fixed amount. Once the pot is full, the contract pays the next member and starts the next round.

03

Take your turn. Or take it back.

Each member receives one pot per cycle. If an incomplete round times out, the circle ends and contributors can claim that round's payment back.
